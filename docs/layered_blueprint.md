# Layered Blueprint of the 'World Simulation'

## Physical Substrate
Description of the physical layer that underpins the simulation, including hardware and environmental considerations.

## Information Infrastructure
Overview of the systems and technologies that support data processing and communication within the simulation.

## Observation
Details on how data is collected from the environment and its importance in informing simulation processes.

## Action
Explanation of the decision-making processes and actions taken based on observed data.

## Integration
Discussion on how various components of the simulation interact and work together to achieve objectives.

## Knowledge Transmission
Insights into how knowledge is shared among components and systems within the simulation.

## Synthesis
Description of how information is synthesized to form a coherent understanding of the environment.

## Human Context
Exploration of the role of human involvement in the simulation and its impact on outcomes.
